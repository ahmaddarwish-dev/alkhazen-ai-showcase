
# Alkhazen AI

### AI-Powered Accounting Assistant for ERP Systems

Alkhazen AI is an intelligent accounting assistant integrated with Alkhazen ERP, designed to help users explore business data and obtain accounting insights through natural-language questions.

## Overview

The system bridges conversational AI with structured business information, making accounting data easier to access and understand.

## Key Capabilities

- Natural-language interaction with accounting data
- AI-assisted financial and business data analysis
- Integration with the existing Alkhazen ERP ecosystem
- Contextual answers to business and accounting questions
- Simplified exploration of complex business information

## Engineering Focus

- AI Integration
- ERP Integration
- SQL and Structured Data
- Backend Systems
- Business Intelligence Workflows
